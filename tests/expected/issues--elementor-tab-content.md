This release introduces a new licensing flow. Follow the steps in the tabs below to request or renew your license.

Step 1: Request or renew

Step 2: Configure the online environment

Open the license portal and submit a request for the new version. Existing customers can renew from the same screen. Approval usually takes one business day.

After approval, import the license into the online environment and confirm that the modules are activated. Restart the application to apply the changes.

If any step fails, contact support with your license reference number.
